I've re-created an old effect of Rocket flame on Somnium Space Hover bikes

To doit I've created custom shaders using HLSL and CG on Unity to create a realistic flame and movement, and also some vertex displacement, to make it energetic and powerful, and also I've created an extra effect with Visual Effect, to create the dust and also the heat distortion
