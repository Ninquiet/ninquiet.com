A set of Custom GPTs designed, tested, and iterated in public. The most used one, **Unity Dev Assistant**, helps developers with Unity coding and game development questions.

In July 2026 they crossed **400K+ users**.

## What's next

OpenAI is retiring custom GPTs on December 11, 2026, and moving creators to plugins. My Unity work in ChatGPT now goes into [Unity Essentials](/projects/unity-essentials/), a free plugin with Unity engineering workflows for Codex. You can [try it in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugins_6a5bb1f60cec8191ad25c3c57abba544).
