A dash that moves you to your destination instead of the typical VR teleport.

## Problem

Classic teleportation is comfortable but breaks the sense of moving through the world. I wanted something more dynamic.

## Approach

Instead of jumping instantly, the player dashes quickly to the chosen destination, keeping a sense of travel.

## VR constraints

Artificial movement can cause discomfort in VR, so the dash has to be quick and controlled to keep the feeling of motion without making people sick.
